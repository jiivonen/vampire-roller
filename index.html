    Reading the roll

    • 6 or higher is a success. A 10 counts as a success.
    • Each pair of 10s is a critical and adds two extra successes.
    • A hunger 10 inside that critical makes it a messy critical.
    • A hunger 1 is a potential bestial failure. If the roll has no successes, it is a bestial failure.
